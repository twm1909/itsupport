Frequently Asked Questions

Go to the Self-Service Password Portal from any corporate device and choose “Reset network password”. You will be asked to verify your identity with your registered multi-factor authentication method. If you are locked out completely or off the network, call the Service Desk hotline and an agent will verify you and issue a temporary password that you must change at next logon.

First response targets are based on the priority you select: Critical within 1 hour, High within 4 hours, Medium within 1 business day and Low within 3 business days. Resolution time depends on the complexity of the issue and whether parts, approvals or third parties are involved. You will receive email updates as the ticket progresses, and you can reply to any update to add information.

Open the corporate VPN client from your Start menu or Applications folder and sign in with your network username and password. Approve the multi-factor authentication prompt on your registered device to complete the connection. If the client is missing or fails to install, raise a ticket under “Network & VPN” and include your device name.

Hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a full shutdown, then disconnect any dock, adapters and USB devices and try again on charger power. If you still see no display or a diagnostic error code, note the code and raise a ticket under “Hardware”. For a Critical deadline, call the hotline so we can arrange a loaner device.

Submit a ticket under “Software / Application” with the product name, version and a short business justification. Your line manager will be asked to approve, and the Service Desk will confirm licensing and security clearance before packaging it for deployment. Approved standard applications are usually pushed to your device within two business days.

Do not click any links, open attachments or reply. Use the “Report Phishing” button in your mail client, or forward the message to the phishing mailbox, then delete it. If you have already clicked or entered any details, raise a ticket under “Security Incident” immediately and call the hotline.

Raise a ticket under “Account & Access” and specify the exact drive path or application name and the level of access you need. The request is routed to the data or system owner for approval, which keeps access aligned with least-privilege policy. Once approved, access is normally granted within one business day and you will get a confirmation email.

Use the reference number from your confirmation (format UOB-ITSD-YYYYMMDD-####) to look up the ticket in the Service Desk portal, where you can see its status and history. You can add a comment or attachment at any time, or simply reply to one of the ticket’s email notifications. To escalate, mark the update as urgent or call the hotline with your reference number.
