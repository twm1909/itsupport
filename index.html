    03  /  Self-service runbook

    Fix it faster

    Common issues the desk resolves every day. Search, or open an entry to see the steps.

    Open the Self-Service Password Portal from any corporate device and choose “Reset network password”. You’ll verify your identity with your registered multi-factor method. If you’re fully locked out or off the network, call the hotline — an agent will verify you and issue a temporary password that you must change at next sign-in.

    First-response targets follow the priority you pick: Critical within 1 hour, High within 4 hours, Medium within 1 business day, Low within 3 business days. Resolution time depends on the issue — whether parts, approvals or a third party are involved. You’ll get email updates as it progresses, and you can reply to any update to add detail.

    Open the corporate VPN client from your Start menu or Applications folder and sign in with your network username and password. Approve the multi-factor prompt on your registered device to finish connecting. If the client is missing or won’t install, raise a ticket under “Network & VPN” and include your device name.

    Hold the power button for 15 seconds to force a full shutdown, then disconnect any dock, adapters and USB devices and try again on charger power. If you still get no display or see a diagnostic error code, note the code and raise a ticket under “Hardware”. For a Critical deadline, call the hotline so we can arrange a loaner device.

    Raise a ticket under “Software / Application” with the product name, version and a short business justification. Your line manager is asked to approve, and the desk confirms licensing and security clearance before packaging it for deployment. Approved standard applications usually reach your device within two business days.

    Don’t click any links, open attachments or reply. Use the “Report Phishing” button in your mail client, or forward the message to the phishing mailbox, then delete it. If you’ve already clicked or entered any details, raise a ticket under “Security Incident” straight away and call the hotline.

    Raise a ticket under “Account & Access” and give the exact drive path or application name and the level of access you need. It’s routed to the data or system owner for approval, which keeps access aligned with least-privilege policy. Once approved, access is normally granted within one business day and you get a confirmation email.

    Use the reference from your confirmation (format UOB-ITSD-YYYYMMDD-####) to look the ticket up in the service desk portal, where you can see its status and history. Add a comment or attachment any time, or just reply to one of the ticket’s email notifications. To escalate, mark the update urgent or call the hotline with your reference number.
